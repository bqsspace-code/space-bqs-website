## 1. The Core Strategic Wedge: Why Competitors Are Failing at SEO

Most competitors in the marine fuel sector fall into two polar extremes:
1. **The Software Utility Trap:** Tools like *CargoSurveyor* or *MMS* build pure native apps and neglect web search entirely. They rely 100% on App Store search, forfeiting enterprise procurement traffic from survey firm managing directors.
2. **The Corporate Inspection Trap:** Giants like *VPS*, *SGS*, and *Intertek* produce corporate brochures and gated PDF whitepapers. Their websites are not structured with clean programmatic landing pages, interactive calculators, or searchable ASTM table lookups.
3. **The Unstructured Technical Blog Trap:** Informational sites like *SevenSurveyor* and *TheNavalArch* rank well organically because they publish raw formulas and Excel files, but they suffer from high bounce rates, zero SaaS conversion funnels, and outdated UX.

**Space BQS's Strategic Wedge:**  
Capture the high-intent calculation and compliance search traffic that *SevenSurveyor* currently dominates, pair it with the software credibility of an enterprise SaaS platform, and route visitors through interactive web tools into pilot team demo bookings.
