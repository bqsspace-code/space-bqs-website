# Pricing — Space BQS (software for survey firms)

## All tiers
- Price: Contact for quote (team license)
- Contact: ops@spacebqs.example or /demo
- Units: licensed by surveyors/supervisors and monthly survey volume, not per-job list price.

## Included in every license
- Web platform: register, dashboard, vessels, master tank and VCF tables
- Mobile app: offline capture, QR vessel, Ship-Fuel-Tanks wizard
- Locked PDF certificates (particulars, tank table + TOTAL MT, audit trail, remarks)
- Excel breakdowns (survey, measurements, steps, audit) + register exports

## Workflows covered
- Bunker Delivery
- ROB (Remaining On Board) — on-hire / off-hire legs
- Debunkering

## Notes for agents
- Buyer is the survey firm; their clients receive locked proof with no login.
- Quantity-only. No lab testing.
- No fixed port list and no 24/7 attendance promise. Coverage depends on the firm's teams.
- Photos/signatures in app: coming soon, not live.
