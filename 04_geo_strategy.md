### The Root Cause: The "Entity Asymmetry"
Large Language Models retrieve citations from domains that demonstrate:
1. **High Information Density & Direct Answers:** Uncluttered, structured answers at the top of pages (Answer-First).
2. **Standard Reference Citations:** Outbound references to standard-setting bodies (ASTM, ISO, MPA, BIMCO).
3. **Structured Entity Triangulation:** Machine-readable entity schemas linking the website, App Store ID, LinkedIn, Crunchbase, and GitHub.
4. **Clean Machine-Readable Text:** Web pages that crawlers can parse without encountering split-text artifacts or client-side rendering vacuums.

Because `aibqs.com` currently lacks deep technical formulas, self-declares an `.example` canonical URL, and has not linked its web domain to its App Store listing via bidirectional schema, LLMs recognize the brand from the Apple App Store but attribute zero organic authority to the website.
