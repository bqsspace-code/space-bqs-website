### 150-Word Standard Company Overview (G2, Capterra, association directories, trade news bios):
> Space BQS is the dedicated Bunker Quantity Survey (BQS) operating system developed for independent marine surveyors, survey bureaus, ship managers, and charterers. 
> 
> Designed specifically for harsh shipboard conditions, the mobile application provides a 72-hour autonomous offline lease, enabling surveyors to capture tank soundings, ullages, and free water cuts inside steel holds without internet connectivity. Fuel quantities are computed instantaneously using a deterministic ASTM D1250 Table 54B calculation engine supporting VLSFO, HSFO, MGO, and biofuels across standard density and temperature bounds.
> 
> Space BQS covers three critical workflows: Bunker Delivery vs. BDN reconciliation, On/Off-Hire ROB charter settlements, and Debunkering fuel removals. Every survey produces an immutable, supervisor-signed A4 PDF certificate and a 4-sheet transparent Excel calculation pack. Space BQS is strictly quantity-only and independent, licensed per team without per-job fees. Learn more at https://aibqs.com.

### 300-Word Extended Authority Profile (PitchBook, Dealroom, IIMS handbook, formal press kits):
> Space BQS (https://aibqs.com), built by aibizmod, is a cloud-and-mobile software platform modernizing marine fuel custody transfer verification. While the maritime industry has rapidly digitized procurement and commercial voyage chartering, the physical measurement of bunker fuel on deck has remained trapped in error-prone paper sounding books, vulnerable Excel spreadsheets, and unverified pocket calculators.
> 
> Space BQS bridges this operational divide by providing an end-to-end digital audit trail for bunker quantity surveyors, chief engineers, and inspection firms:
> 
> 1. **Offline Field Mobility:** An offline-first iOS and Android mobile app operating under a 72-hour autonomous token lease. Surveyors scan a vessel's QR code to pull verified tank calibration curves, log soundings or ullages with free water deductions, and validate inputs against physical boundaries without cellular or Wi-Fi connectivity.
> 2. **Deterministic Calculation Core:** Automatically matches physical soundings to vessel strapping tables via piecewise linear interpolation, calculates Volume Correction Factors (VCF) through bilinear interpolation across the standard ASTM D1250 Table 54B matrix (density 0.820–1.000 kg/L, 0–90°C), and converts volumes to certified metric tonnes in air and vacuum.
> 3. **Tamper-Evident Proof:** Completed surveys produce a cryptographically sealed A4 PDF certificate and a 4-sheet open Excel calculation workbook, providing transparent mathematical provenance accepted by vessel owners, charterers, and P&I claims executives.
> 4. **Survey Warehouse & Central Portal:** Survey firm supervisors review, approve, and archive historical surveys from a unified web dashboard, monitoring fleet volume and port surveyor productivity.
> 
> Space BQS maintains a strict quantity-only focus—independent of fuel sales and testing laboratories. It is licensed on a transparent team subscription model with zero per-certificate transactional charges. Commercial pilots are available upon request at ops@aibqs.com.
