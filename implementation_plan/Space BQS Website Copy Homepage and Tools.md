## 2B. Tool page template (example: Shortfall and cost) `/tools/shortfall-cost`

**Meta title:** Bunker Shortfall Calculator: Tonnes, Percent and Cost | Space BQS

**H1:** Bunker shortfall calculator

**Purpose line:** See how far a measured quantity falls from the delivery note, and what the gap is worth.

**Inputs:** Delivery note quantity (MT) · Measured quantity (MT) · Price per MT (currency selector)

**Outputs:** Difference in MT · Difference in percent · Value of the difference · Plain-English line: “The measured quantity is \[x\] MT (\[y\]%) below the delivery note, worth about \[z\].”

**Buttons:** Copy result · Print · Share this calculation

**How it is calculated (accordion):** Difference = measured − delivery note; percent = difference ÷ delivery note; value = difference × price. No tolerance is applied; check your contract for allowed variance.

**Common mistakes**

- Comparing volume with mass. Convert both to the same basis first.
- Mixing air and vacuum figures.
- Ignoring the contract tolerance.
